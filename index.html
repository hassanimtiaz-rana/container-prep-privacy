Container Prep Privacy Policy

Container Prep: Learn & Quiz is published by Toolify Ai Studio. Android package: com.toolifyaistudio.dockerprep. Contact toolifyaistudio@gmail.com.

Learning records

The app stores lesson completion, bookmarks, review schedules, theme preference, and quiz history on your device. It has no account, ads, purchases, developer analytics, or learning-record backend. The browser preview stores its records in the browser.

Updates and external links

Eligible Android release builds can ask Google Play whether an update is available and display its native update flow. Google Play processes update-related technical metadata under its own policies. Opening official references uses your browser and the destination service’s policies.

Deletion and support

Remove the installed app or clear its application storage to delete on-device records. Clear the site’s browser storage to delete preview records. Records are not synchronized by the app. If you email support, your email address and message are used to respond and retained as needed to resolve the request and maintain support records. Request deletion through the support address.

Permissions and changes

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, or contacts access. This policy will be updated if the app’s data handling changes.
